## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Proof that you are enrolled, or have been accepted, at [[type of institution]].
- Your most recent grades or transcript.
- [[Any other document, for example proof of household income.]]

Each program page lists anything extra.

## Step by step

1. **Pick a program.** Read the program page and check the deadline. Deadlines are exact and name their time zone.
2. **Check that you qualify.** The "Who can apply" list on each program page is the whole test. If you are unsure, [contact us](/contact/) before you apply.
3. **Create an account.** You use it to save your application and come back to it. [[How accounts work, to be confirmed.]]
4. **Fill in the form and upload your documents.** [[Say whether applicants can save and finish later.]]
5. **Wait for our decision.** We tell every applicant the result by email by [[date or number of weeks]].
